🔬 Clinical Interpretation of Results
🟢Stage 4 Prediction (F1 {{ per_class[3].f1 }}%): The model is most reliable for end-stage cirrhosis detection — the clinically most urgent case. High bilirubin, low albumin, and prolonged prothrombin time are strong predictors.
🟡Stage 3 Prediction (F1 {{ per_class[2].f1 }}%): Advanced fibrosis is detected reasonably well. Alkaline phosphatase and copper levels are key discriminators at this stage.
🔴Stage 1 Prediction (F1 {{ per_class[0].f1 }}%): Early fibrosis is nearly impossible to detect with only 21 training samples. A larger dataset with 500+ Stage 1 patients would dramatically improve early detection.
💡 Model Strengths & Limitations
✅Strength: Random Forest handles mixed data types (numeric + categorical), is robust to outliers in lab values, and provides built-in feature importance for clinical interpretability.
✅Strength: Ensemble of 200 trees reduces variance from individual overfitted trees. More robust than a single Decision Tree or Logistic Regression on this small dataset.
⚠️Limitation: 412 patients is a small clinical dataset. Cross-validated accuracy of {{ cv_mean }}% is honest and expected. A multi-centre dataset (5,000+ patients) would significantly improve all metrics.
