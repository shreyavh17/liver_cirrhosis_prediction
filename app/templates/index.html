About HepatoAI

HepatoAI is a Final Year B.E. Project developed at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Sharnbasva University, Kalaburagi. It applies a Random Forest ensemble machine learning classifier to predict the stage of liver cirrhosis (Stages 1–4) from routine clinical and laboratory data, providing non-invasive decision support for hepatology clinicians.

Core AlgorithmRandom Forest (200 Trees)
